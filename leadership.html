Shubham Saraff

Co Founder and Director, STAIR Digital

LinkedIn saraff@stair.digital

Shubham is Co Founder of STAIR Digital, with deep expertise in Exponential Organisations, AI driven business transformation and venture investing. He is an MBA alumnus of Saïd Business School, Oxford, a former Chairperson of the Technology Business Network at Oxford University, and an Investment Partner at Oxford Syndicate Capital, focusing on ESG and technology based venture investments.

He advises the Managing Director and CEO of one of India's largest listed fintechs and serves as CIO to a venture capital fund, its investee companies and startups, on future ready technologies, strategy and brand positioning. He was the youngest ever Board Advisor for Technology Policy and AI at the IET, the global technology body of more than 160,000 professionals across 150 countries.

With 10 years of research in AI, neural networks and machine learning, he has published in international journals and received the Best Research Paper Award by Cambridge and the IET Global Award. He is a TEDx speaker on artificial intelligence and human connectedness at Oxford and Bocconi, and a frequent speaker, moderator and jury member at technology panels.
