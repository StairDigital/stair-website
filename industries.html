Where we work

Six sectors. One operating standard.

We do not arrive with a generic playbook. Each sector carries its own regulator, its own margin logic and its own tolerance for risk, so the governance and the AI are built to fit it.

Financial Services

Banks, NBFCs, insurers and capital markets

Regulation moves fastest here, and AI is watched hardest. We put governance around model risk, harden the audit trail, and put agents on the reconciliation and compliance load that consumes the close.

  • Model risk governance
  • RBI and SEBI LODR alignment
  • Fraud and AML controls
  • Financial close automation

Manufacturing & Industrials

Plants, supply chains and diversified groups

Multi plant groups run on data that rarely reaches the board in time. We connect the plant floor to the boardroom, then apply AI where throughput, quality and working capital actually move.

  • Plant to board reporting
  • Predictive quality and maintenance
  • Supply chain visibility
  • Operating model redesign

Healthcare & Life Sciences

Providers, pharma and diagnostics

Clinical and regulatory stakes leave no room for unmanaged AI. We govern every point a model touches a patient, and put the agents on the documentation and coding burden instead.

  • Clinical AI governance
  • Regulatory documentation
  • Patient data protection
  • Revenue cycle automation

Energy & Infrastructure

Utilities, renewables and national assets

Assets with thirty year horizons answering to quarterly scrutiny. We build the control and assurance layer that lets capital committees commit with confidence, and keep the reporting audit ready.

  • Asset performance intelligence
  • Capex governance
  • ESG and regulatory reporting
  • Grid and network analytics

Retail & Consumer

Brands, distribution and consumer platforms

Margin lives in demand accuracy and inventory discipline. We install decision intelligence where the numbers change daily rather than quarterly, and let agents hold the long tail of the operation.

  • Demand forecasting
  • Pricing and margin intelligence
  • Distribution analytics
  • Customer facing agents

Technology & Telecom

Platforms, carriers and digital infrastructure

Fast moving businesses that still have to answer to a board. We institutionalise them without slowing them down, and get the governance to the standard capital markets expect before the raise, not after.

  • Pre IPO readiness
  • Platform governance
  • Network and usage intelligence
  • Agent based operations
The thinking underneath
